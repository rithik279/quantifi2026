## Known limitations of the submitted file
- **The options book is only partly delta-hedged.** LYCHEE quotes are skewed toward the hedge position, but LYCHEE is soft-capped at 40 and the skew is only 3 coins at that cap. Measured on days −2 and 0, the vouchers' net delta reaches about 110, while LYCHEE never holds more than 20. Most strikes end each day at the ±50 limit. So the book carries most of its delta, and it's better described as "hedge-skewed" than "delta-hedged".
- **Errors are swallowed silently.** `Trader.run` wraps each strategy family in `except Exception: pass`. If a family fails on the official site, it just stops trading, and nothing is written to `traderData`. (The `<group>_error` reporting described below exists only in files generated by `build_trader.py`, such as `trader_plain.py`.)

## How to read the differences

- **Upload 1 matches (within a few %):** our fill model is right, and the numbers in `notebooks/*/NOTES.md` can be trusted as *their* backtest numbers.
- **Upload 1 is lower:** their engine fills us less often than ours. Compare uploads 2–5 against the fill-stress table in `notebooks/robustness/NOTES.md` (fill rate 75% / 50% / 25%, and "only trades through our price"). The row that matches best becomes our planning assumption, and we re-check parameters under it. Locally only TELLURIUM and AMETHYST are sensitive to this; the taking-based products (options, TOFFEE_CAKE, PAPER_LANTERNS, PINK, basket) barely move.
- **Options way off but everything else fine:** it's *not* the day issue. Assuming day 1 throughout costs only 0.1k locally. Check for errors or timeouts instead (options is the heaviest per tick), and send me the numbers.
- **Any product crashes:** the generated combined trader (`build_trader.py` output) catches errors per group and keeps the others trading, then stores the error text in `traderData` under `<group>_error`. The submitted v10 file does not record errors (see Known limitations above).
